# Role: Clinical Outcomes Framework

## Objective

Provide a role-appropriate capability framework for healthcare roles that translates experience into screening-ready impact.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Patient Outcomes & Safety**

- Demonstrates measurable improvements in patient outcomes or safety indicators
- Uses evidence-based practice and risk mitigation

2. **Clinical Excellence & Protocol Adherence**

- Operates consistently within clinical protocols and standards
- Maintains high-quality documentation and accuracy

3. **Operational Efficiency**

- Improves workflow, throughput, handoffs, and care coordination
- Reduces wait time, errors, rework, and bottlenecks

4. **Communication & Interdisciplinary Collaboration**

- Effective handoffs, escalation, and stakeholder alignment (patients/families/teams)
- Works well across nursing, physicians, allied health, and admin

5. **Compliance & Quality**

- HIPAA/privacy, infection prevention, audit readiness, incident response
- Quality improvement participation and sustained adherence

## Language Transformations

| Generic | Clinical Outcomes |
|---------|-------------------|
| Provided patient care | Delivered patient-centered care improving [outcome/proxy] while maintaining safety standards |
| Assisted procedures | Supported clinical procedures with strict protocol adherence and accurate documentation |
| Worked with team | Coordinated interdisciplinary care plans across X stakeholders for safe transitions |
| Improved process | Reduced handoff errors by X% through standardized checklists and training |

## Outcome Metrics & Proxies

- Readmission reduction, falls reduction, medication error reduction
- Patient satisfaction (HCAHPS/NPS proxies), time-to-triage, length of stay proxies
- Throughput (patients/day), documentation timeliness, compliance/audit findings

# Power Language Guide: A+++ Resume Optimization

## Purpose

This guide ensures every resume produced by Career Architect passes screening stages by using **precise, technical, domain-specific language** that signals expertise to both ATS systems and human recruiters.

---

## The 6-Second Rule

Recruiters spend 6 seconds on initial scan. Your resume must:

1. **Lead with impact** - First bullet of each role = biggest win
2. **Front-load numbers** - Put metrics at the start of bullets
3. **Use power verbs** - Action-first, never passive voice
4. **Mirror JD language** - Exact keyword matching for ATS

---

## Universal Power Principles

### 1. Specificity Beats Generality

| ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---------|-----------|
| Improved performance | Reduced P95 latency from 450ms to 89ms |
| Managed large budget | Controlled $2.4M annual budget with 3% variance |
| Led team | Directed cross-functional team of 8 across 3 time zones |
| Increased sales | Closed $1.2M in net-new ARR, 140% of quota |

### 2. Technical Precision Signals Expertise

| ❌ Vague | ✅ Technical |
|----------|-------------|
| Built database | Architected PostgreSQL schema with GIN indexes for sub-10ms full-text search |
| Set up cloud | Provisioned multi-AZ Kubernetes cluster handling 50K RPS with 99.99% uptime |
| Made reports | Built Looker dashboards with real-time CDC from Snowflake, used by C-suite weekly |
| Ran campaigns | Orchestrated multi-touch ABM campaigns via 6sense + Marketo integration |

### 3. Outcome Hierarchy (Most to Least Impressive)

1. **Revenue generated** - "$X in new revenue"
2. **Cost saved** - "Reduced costs by $X"
3. **Time saved** - "Cut cycle time by X%"
4. **Scale achieved** - "Scaled to X users/transactions"
5. **Quality improved** - "Reduced errors by X%"
6. **Process created** - "Established X, adopted by Y teams"

## ATS Optimization Rules

### Keyword Matching

1. **Extract exact phrases** from JD (e.g., "cross-functional collaboration" not just "collaboration")
2. **Include acronyms AND full terms** (e.g., "Customer Relationship Management (CRM)")
3. **Match their stack exactly** (e.g., if JD says "React", don't write "ReactJS")
4. **Use industry-standard terminology** even if company uses internal jargon

### Format Rules for ATS

1. **No tables, columns, or graphics** - ATS can't parse them
2. **Standard section headers** - Experience, Education, Skills (not "My Journey")
3. **Dates in consistent format** - "Jan 2022 - Present" or "2022 - Present"
4. **Skills as comma-separated list** - not nested bullets

### Keyword Density

- **Primary keywords** (role title, core skills): Appear 3-5 times
- **Secondary keywords** (tools, methodologies): Appear 2-3 times
- **Tertiary keywords** (soft skills, values): Appear 1-2 times

## Bullet Formula: The XYZ Method

**Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]**

Examples:
- "Reduced infrastructure costs by 40% ($180K annually) by migrating from EC2 to spot instances with intelligent scheduling"
- "Increased qualified pipeline by 65% ($2.1M) by implementing intent-based ABM targeting via 6sense"
- "Accelerated month-end close from 12 days to 5 by automating 80% of reconciliation workflows in NetSuite"

## Summary Statement Formula

**[Title/Identity] with [X years] experience [core expertise]. Proven track record of [biggest achievement with metric]. Expert in [3-4 key skills matching JD].**

Examples:

**Engineering:**
"Staff Software Engineer with 10+ years architecting distributed systems at scale. Led platform migration handling 50M daily transactions with 99.99% uptime. Expert in Kubernetes, event-driven architecture, and building high-performance engineering teams."

**Sales:**
"Enterprise Account Executive with 8+ years closing complex SaaS deals in Fortune 500 accounts. Consistently exceeded quota (avg. 135% attainment), closing $4.2M in net-new ARR in FY24. Expert in MEDDIC, multi-threaded selling, and C-suite engagement."

**Marketing:**
"Demand Generation Leader with 7+ years building scalable B2B pipelines. Generated $12M in attributable pipeline at <$50 CAC through integrated ABM and content strategies. Expert in Marketo, 6sense, and full-funnel optimization."

## Red Flags That Fail Screening

### Instant Rejections
- ❌ No metrics (looks like task list, not achievements)
- ❌ Passive voice ("Was responsible for...")
- ❌ First person ("I managed...")
- ❌ Buzzwords without substance ("Results-oriented team player")
- ❌ Gaps unexplained (address in cover letter if needed)
- ❌ Job hopping without progression narrative

### Weak Signals
- ❌ Generic verbs (Helped, Assisted, Worked on, Participated)
- ❌ Vague scope ("large team", "significant budget")
- ❌ Missing tools/technologies (suggests surface-level knowledge)
- ❌ Education-first layout (for experienced candidates)

# Role: Creative Portfolio Framework

## Objective

Provide a role-appropriate capability framework for creative roles (design, writing, brand, content) that translates experience into credible, screening-ready impact.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Craft & Execution Quality**

- Produces high-quality creative outputs with strong taste and consistency
- Demonstrates mastery of tools and workflows

2. **User/Audience Understanding**

- Uses research, insight, and feedback loops to guide creative decisions
- Connects creative choices to audience needs and behaviors

3. **Narrative & Communication**

- Frames work with clear problem → approach → outcome storytelling
- Communicates rationale, tradeoffs, and intent

4. **Systems & Consistency**

- Builds reusable systems (design systems, editorial standards, brand guidelines)
- Enables scale through templates, components, and governance

5. **Business Impact**

- Connects creative outputs to measurable outcomes
- Common proxies: conversion rate, engagement, retention, NPS, adoption, CTR, brand lift

## Language Transformations

| Generic | Creative Portfolio |
|---------|--------------------|
| Designed X | Shaped end-to-end experience for X, improving [metric] by Y% |
| Wrote content | Produced narrative assets driving [channel metric] by Y% |
| Made brand updates | Refined brand system across X touchpoints, improving consistency and recognition |
| Worked with team | Partnered cross-functionally to align creative direction with product and GTM goals |

## Output

Use this framework to:

- Prioritize the most relevant portfolio projects
- Translate subjective “creative work” into measurable outcomes
- Select the right verbs and metrics for resume bullets

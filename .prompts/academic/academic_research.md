# Role: Academic Research Framework

## Objective

Provide a role-appropriate capability framework for academic roles (research, teaching, grants) that translates experience into screening-ready impact.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Research Contributions**

- Novel contributions, methodological rigor, and reproducibility
- Publications, preprints, datasets, or tools

2. **Scholarly Communication**

- Writing clarity, peer review readiness, conference presentations
- Ability to translate research impact to broader audiences

3. **Grant & Funding Readiness**

- Grant writing, proposals, budgeting, compliance
- Collaboration with PIs and institutions

4. **Teaching & Mentorship**

- Teaching philosophy, curriculum design, supervision
- Mentoring students and junior researchers

5. **Service & Collaboration**

- Committee work, departmental service, cross-lab collaboration
- Ethical research practice and governance

## Language Transformations

| Generic | Academic Research |
|---------|-------------------|
| Worked on research | Conducted rigorous research on X, producing Y outputs (publication, dataset, method) |
| Wrote papers | Authored manuscripts resulting in [acceptance/impact proxy] |
| Helped students | Mentored X students, improving outcomes and research throughput |
| Presented work | Presented findings at X venues, driving collaborations |

## Impact Proxies

- Publications, citations, venue quality, replication artifacts
- Grants submitted/awarded, budget managed, collaborations
- Teaching evaluations, course outcomes, mentorship progression

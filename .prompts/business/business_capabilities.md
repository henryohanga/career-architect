# Philosophy: Business Impact Framework

## Core Principles

The Business Impact Framework emphasizes measurable outcomes, stakeholder influence, and operational excellence. Unlike technical roles where system architecture is paramount, business roles are evaluated on their ability to drive revenue, reduce costs, improve processes, and lead people.

## Five Core Capabilities

### 1. Revenue & Growth Ownership

**Definition**: Direct accountability for revenue targets, pipeline growth, or cost optimization.

**Evidence Patterns**:
- Exceeded quota by X%
- Grew pipeline from $X to $Y
- Reduced costs by $X through process improvement
- Increased customer retention by X%

**Language**:
- "Owned $X revenue target, achieving Y% attainment"
- "Drove X% growth in [metric] through [strategy]"
- "Delivered $X in cost savings via [initiative]"

### 2. Stakeholder Influence

**Definition**: Ability to align cross-functional teams, manage up, and drive decisions without direct authority.

**Evidence Patterns**:
- Led cross-functional initiative with X teams
- Presented to C-suite/board on [topic]
- Negotiated with vendors/partners saving $X
- Built consensus across conflicting priorities

**Language**:
- "Aligned X stakeholders across [departments] to deliver [outcome]"
- "Influenced executive decision on [initiative], resulting in [impact]"
- "Navigated competing priorities to secure [resource/approval]"

### 3. Process & Operational Excellence

**Definition**: Designing, implementing, and improving processes that scale.

**Evidence Patterns**:
- Reduced cycle time by X%
- Improved SLA compliance from X% to Y%
- Automated manual process, saving X hours/week
- Implemented system/tool adopted by X users

**Language**:
- "Redesigned [process], reducing [metric] by X%"
- "Implemented [system] across X locations, improving [outcome]"
- "Standardized [workflow], enabling X% faster [activity]"

### 4. Data-Driven Decision Making

**Definition**: Using analytics and insights to inform strategy and measure outcomes.

**Evidence Patterns**:
- Built dashboard/reporting for X stakeholders
- Identified insight that led to $X decision
- Improved forecast accuracy by X%
- A/B tested [initiative], improving [metric] by X%

**Language**:
- "Leveraged [data/analytics] to identify [opportunity], resulting in [outcome]"
- "Built reporting framework adopted by X teams for [purpose]"
- "Improved forecast accuracy from X% to Y% through [method]"

### 5. Team Leadership & Development

**Definition**: Building, leading, and developing high-performing teams.

**Evidence Patterns**:
- Managed team of X, achieving Y% of targets
- Promoted X team members within Y months
- Reduced turnover from X% to Y%
- Hired and onboarded X people in Y timeframe

**Language**:
- "Led team of X to achieve [target], ranking #Y in [org/region]"
- "Developed X team members into [next role], with Y% promotion rate"
- "Built [function/team] from X to Y people while maintaining [quality metric]"

---

## Transformation Rules

When converting generic language to Business Impact language:

| Generic | Business Impact |
|---------|-----------------|
| Managed project | Drove initiative to completion, delivering $X impact |
| Worked with team | Aligned X stakeholders to achieve [outcome] |
| Responsible for | Owned [metric/outcome], achieving X% of target |
| Helped improve | Implemented [change] resulting in X% improvement |
| Communicated with | Influenced [audience] to [action], enabling [result] |
| Did analysis | Identified [insight] through analysis, leading to [decision] |

## Vertical Adaptations

### Sales Roles
- Emphasize: Quota attainment, deal size, win rate, pipeline velocity
- Metrics: ARR, ACV, conversion rates, sales cycle length
- Language: "Closed", "Negotiated", "Expanded", "Retained"

### Marketing Roles
- Emphasize: Campaign performance, lead generation, brand metrics
- Metrics: CAC, MQL/SQL, engagement rates, ROAS, pipeline contribution
- Language: "Launched", "Optimized", "Scaled", "Positioned"

### Operations Roles
- Emphasize: Efficiency, cost reduction, compliance, scalability
- Metrics: Cycle time, error rates, cost per unit, SLA compliance
- Language: "Streamlined", "Automated", "Standardized", "Optimized"

### Finance Roles
- Emphasize: Accuracy, compliance, strategic insight, cost control
- Metrics: Variance, audit findings, forecast accuracy, savings
- Language: "Analyzed", "Forecasted", "Controlled", "Reported"

### HR/People Roles
- Emphasize: Talent acquisition, retention, engagement, development
- Metrics: Time-to-hire, retention rate, eNPS, training completion
- Language: "Recruited", "Developed", "Implemented", "Engaged"

### Project/Program Management
- Emphasize: On-time delivery, budget adherence, stakeholder satisfaction
- Metrics: On-time %, budget variance, scope completion, NPS
- Language: "Delivered", "Coordinated", "Tracked", "Escalated"

---

## Direct Integration

At least 50% of Experience bullet points should demonstrate one of the Five Capabilities. Each bullet should:

1. Start with a strong action verb
2. Include a specific metric or quantified outcome
3. Show scope (team size, budget, geography, etc.)
4. Connect to business impact (revenue, cost, efficiency, growth)
